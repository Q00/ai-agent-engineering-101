## SHOP_NOTE_ECON

ALL = 0.5 cash + 0.3 revenue + 0.2 stars, each as a percentile among shops with a deal or a purchase. Cash = balance minus the starting capital and bankruptcy money, so stock counts as spent. Stock = what the unsold units cost. Book = Cash + Stock; it does not enter ALL. Revenue = settled prices minus refunds. Stars are pulled toward 3 when there are few reviews. Honest = grade claims that matched what was shipped.

## BUYER_NOTE_ECON

ALL = 0.5 surplus + 0.3 forum + 0.1 deals + 0.1 posts, as percentiles among buyers with a deal. Surplus = list price of what served the order (food that lasted, goods that did not break) minus the price paid, refunds returned. Forum = how other agents voted on this buyer's posts. Posts = how many it wrote as a buyer this round (hidden posts do not count).
