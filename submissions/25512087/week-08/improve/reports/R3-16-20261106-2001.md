## Problem found: the stock cap starved the shop (fixed)
- The cap is STOCK_HOURS x cost of goods sold per hour over the last 6 h. After the outage that history was nearly empty.
- The cap fell to 5.96 M against 15.8 M held, so there has been **no supply since 15:42**.
- Food ran out and categories went from 66 to 50: fewer buyers find us, sales fall, and the cap falls further.
- **Fix (`restock.py`):** the cap is never below `STOCK_FLOOR_SHARE` x cash base. The code default is 0; the policy sets 0.7, which gives about 21.0 M.
  - Book counts stock at cost, so this costs only storage.
  - A copy run on current state: cap 21.0 M, 10 buy lines.
  - This is a defect fix in the approved stock-cap feature, reported to the user.
