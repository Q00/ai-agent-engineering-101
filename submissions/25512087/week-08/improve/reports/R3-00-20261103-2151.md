## What the data says
- **Shop traffic is the bottleneck, not price.** 92 % of the buyers who talk to us sign. Only 12 talks in 49 minutes, though.
- **We hold no food.** Food was pushed down the coverage order. Yet 50 % of our own buyer's orders are food (김치 28, 신선과일 23, 과자 14, of 136).
- **The ledger demand signal was dead.** `.market/memory/restock.json` had no ledger seq. macOS python's urllib has no CA bundle, so it could not read the ledger.
  - Fixed 21:5x: httpx first. Our own buyer's order categories now also feed the demand counts, at half weight.
- **Buyer: 24 of 64 signed deals were not paid.** The kit's accept check refused them:
  - 12 units came without the market certificate.
  - 12 food units would not last the order's `use_within_hours`, so they would have been worth 0.
  - Refusing them was right, but every one of them was a wasted talk.
- **Buyer no-deals are mostly "none in stock".** 36 of 120 talks ended that way: shops.json offers go stale between listing and talking.

## Changes in this cycle
- Code (approved in chat, 11/03):
  - restock reads the ledger with httpx and counts our buyer's order categories.
  - `tuning.py` and `improve/policy.json` hold the knobs and bounds. The seller, restock and buyer read them without a restart; the seller only from its next restart.
  - market_buyer runs the R3 path only when order.json matches the current order.
- Knobs: none. This is the baseline. A window under 1 h is too thin to act on.

## Code proposals (need approval)
1. **Buyer, food:** read "N h shelf life left" from the shop's first reply. Walk at once if it is under `use_within_hours`. That saves the talk and the shop's time, against the 12 refusals above.
2. **Buyer, certificates:** remember shops whose unit came without a certificate and stop choosing them for a day.
3. **Buyer, stale stock:** after a "none in stock" reply, retry the next shop on the shortlist, as is already done for errors.
