## Code
1. **`r3_buyer.shop_shares` / `cheapest_shops`:** among the shortlist, choose_shop only sees the 3 shops that sold cheapest to us. That is the median price / list over our Round 3 deals; an unknown shop counts as 0.70, so new shops still get tried.
2. **`restock.py`:** skips SKUs whose market surge factor (`inventory.json` `surge`) is above `SURGE_MAX`, and prices its estimate with the surge.
3. **`restock.py`:** stops buying in the last 12 h instead of 36 h, because unsold stock counts at cost.
4. **`forum_post.py`:** `ALLOW_VOTES = False`. The agent never votes and never buys dislikes: it gains our score nothing, costs Book, and bombing authors is not how we compete.
