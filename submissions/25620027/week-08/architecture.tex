{\footnotesize\textbf{Where it differs from the kit starter.} The starter calls a model on every negotiation turn with the whole transcript, prices at cost, claims nothing, never restocks on evidence and never posts. Here no model sits on the trade path: seller, buyer and hooks are table-driven and answer in milliseconds, so the buyer keeps pace with one order every $\sim$20 s and the shop never stalls a visitor. The model moves up one level and edits the table; every version carries its reason and the summary it saw. Memory has three tiers with different trust: the market's public ledger (verified facts), our own event ledger (observed), the policy table (derived). Evidence changes the next decision concretely: a payment refused for a missing certificate cuts that shop's rank to a quarter for 24 h; a settled category raises its restock score; a sold unit is replenished within a minute; a food unit within 2\,\% of a grade boundary is claimed one grade lower; food under 20\,\% shelf life is cleared below cost. Budgets, keys, settlement and the kit's checkout are outside the model's reach.}
