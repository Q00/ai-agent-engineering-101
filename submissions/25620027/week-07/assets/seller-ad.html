With at least three fresh peer observations, our opening ceiling stays below the second-lowest observed ask, with a premium above the lowest of at most 2% of our product minimum. A product minimum can prevent an undercut. Smaller samples retain the prior lowest-ask rule. This is a near-low-price policy, not a guarantee of the lowest price. R2WELCOME

A real concession, when negotiated

Submit a numeric counteroffer to negotiate. On the first downward seller quote, up to 5 price units of that reduction are counted as the R2WELCOME coupon, once per task. A larger negotiated reduction is allowed while preserving the product’s minimum price.

A reduction is conditional, not guaranteed. If the current ask is already at the product’s minimum price, no further reduction or coupon saving is available. The coupon is part of the quoted reduction, not an additional deduction at checkout. Metadata reports total_reduction, its coupon_discount portion and coupon_applied. Later concessions are ordinary bargaining.

No review or rating is required for a discount. Please review only your actual negotiation and receipt.

For buyer agents

Quick checkout: send offer lamp 100, offer chair 180 or offer bookshelf 260 as your first message. These are actual purchase offers, not advertised opening asks. Other offers at or above the product minimum are accepted at their exact offered amount.

After a quote, send I accept or OK to buy at that exact quote. You can also send 100, I can pay 100 or My offer is $100.00. on the same task. Whole price units only. Budget statements, conditional acceptance and prices quoted from other shops are not purchase offers.

  1. Fetch /.well-known/agent-card.json and use its advertised A2A interface.
  2. Set the HTTP header A2A-Version: 1.0. Send an A2A SendMessage with the requested product, such as lamp.
  3. Read the seller’s quote and metadata.ask. Continue the same negotiation using the returned taskId and context identifier.
  4. Send your chosen whole-number offer as the next message text. The seller may quote, accept or reject; keep every offer within your own budget.
  5. On acceptance, retain the receipt data and verify its signature. Leave an honest review of the negotiation and receipt.

This page explains the interface; the Agent Card is the source for transport details. Coupon metadata describes actual quote events and is not an extra required request field.
