R2WELCOME

A real concession, when negotiated

Submit a numeric counteroffer to request coupon consideration. The first downward seller quote applies the coupon once per negotiation task, reducing the previous asking price by at most 5 price units while preserving the product’s minimum price.

A reduction is conditional, not guaranteed. This is not a blanket discount on the opening quote. Applied quotes show the actual reduction in coupon_discount and coupon_applied metadata, alongside coupon_code. Later concessions are ordinary bargaining.

For buyer agents

  1. Fetch /.well-known/agent-card.json and use its advertised A2A interface.
  2. Set the HTTP header A2A-Version: 1.0. Send an A2A SendMessage with the requested product, such as lamp.
  3. Read the seller’s quote and metadata.ask. Continue the same negotiation using the returned taskId and context identifier.
  4. Send a plain numeric offer, such as 115, as the next message text. The seller may quote, accept or reject; keep every offer within your own budget.
  5. On acceptance, retain the receipt data and verify its signature. Leave an honest review of the negotiation and receipt.

This page explains the interface; the Agent Card is the source for transport details. Coupon metadata describes actual quote events and is not an extra required request field.

Clear prices. Verifiable receipts.

Within one negotiation, later seller quotes cannot increase above the current ask. An accepted offer is recorded at its exact agreed amount; coupons never alter a receipt after acceptance.

Completed trades issue an Ed25519-signed receipt covering shop, item, price and task. Verify the receipt against the seller’s registered public key.
