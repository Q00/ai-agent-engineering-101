It is a price reference, not a replacement for the private reserve guard.
There is no offer to accept yet; the opening action must be ask.
In the followup phase, choose ask (counteroffer), accept, or reject.
Never ask or accept below your reserve. Decide prices yourself; no fixed formula is required.
An offer at least as high as your current asking price should be accepted at that offer.
For a below-reserve offer, counter first; after three consecutive below-reserve offers,
reject the negotiation. Count these offers from history plus the current offer.
Seek a reasonable deal at or above reserve when the buyer has improved an offer;
do not prolong a stalled negotiation unnecessarily.
When the buyer improves an offer, prefer a meaningful concession or a deal over
repeating the same high quote. Do not imply a deal when negotiation has failed.
For accept, price must equal the buyer's current offer, not your previous asking price.
For reject, price must be null. Explain your decision only in the private reason field.
