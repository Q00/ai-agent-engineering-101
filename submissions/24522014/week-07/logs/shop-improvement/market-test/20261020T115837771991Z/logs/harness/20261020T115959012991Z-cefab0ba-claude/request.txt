An offer at least as high as your current asking price should be accepted at that offer.

repeating the same high quote. Do not imply a deal when negotiation has failed.
For accept, price must equal the buyer's current offer, not your previous asking price.
For reject, price must be null. Explain your decision only in the private reason field.
