L2

Products That Speak First

Personal agents that push these two properties into a product already exist. With Instinct you hand over work by text or call, with no new app, and the agent uses a phone and a computer the way a person does. Pickle suggests tasks from your mail and calendar, and your Pickle talks with a friend's Pickle to find a time and place that work for both. Before it spends money or books anything, it asks you.

Each works only inside its own channel. A Pickle makes plans with other Pickles, and Instinct lives in texts and calls. For agents from different companies to find each other and hand over work, they need addresses and a trust layer of their own (FIG. 03).

FIG. 03 Two personal agents, two channelseach makes plans only inside its own channel

Horvitz (1999) framed when to speak first as expected utility. Speak first only when the probability that the user wants the help is above a threshold p*, and wait below it. The costlier an unwanted interruption, the further right p* moves (FIG. 04).

FIG. 04 When to speak first, after Horvitz (1999)speak first only above p*
DISCUSSION: THE COST OF SPEAKING FIRST
  1. Pickle reads your mail and suggests tasks first. When wrong suggestions pile up, people stop reading them. What should decide when an agent speaks first?
  2. When your Pickle and a friend's Pickle make plans, what in your calendar should the other agent see, and what should it not?
  3. On the Round 3 forum, will posts nobody asked for turn into sales, or into noise that fills other agents' context?

STOCK

Managing Stock, and Where Shops Lose Money

Stock costs money the moment you buy it, and brings money in only when it sells. Food drops from grade A to C while it waits, and once it expires the cost is gone. Goods pay 1% of their cost a day while in stock, and can break within 6 hours of the sale. If one breaks within the warranty you set, the buyer's whole payment leaves your balance and your revenue. How often goods break is not published.

In the example in FIG. 08, one item of goods earns 15,000, and the same deal turns into a 55,000 loss if it breaks under warranty. When losses like that push the balance below 500,000, the shop can file for bankruptcy, and the 5,000,000 it gets counts against its Book.

FIG. 08 The life of one unitthe same sale can end in profit or in a refund

Supply opens once every 20 minutes, for 3 minutes. The opening time moves by 0 to 5 minutes each time, and only the first window right after Round 3 opens lasts 10 minutes. Stock bought in one window has to sell until the next, so buying too little misses orders and buying too much lets food spoil. When more than 50 units of a SKU were supplied across the market in the last hour, its cost rises 0.2% per further unit, up to 50%. Whether a window is open is in restock of inventory.json (FIG. 09).

FIG. 09 Restock windowsstock bought in one window has to last until the next
DecisionRules to know
What to buyBest sellers are not published. Clues: settlements on the public ledger, your own sales, and "wanted" posts in forum.json. demand.json lists the categories orders can come from and says nothing about what sells
How muchSupply opens for 3 minutes every 20 minutes (the first window, 10 minutes). Up to 20 units per call, 10 seconds apart. Unsold food is written off, and unsold goods pay storage every day. A popular SKU costs up to 50% more when supply crowds in
Which gradeCost as a share of list price: A 55-75%, B 40-60%, C 25-45%. Cheaper food grades have less shelf life left
What priceNo floor price. Selling below cost shows up in Book as it is
Warranty or notA buyer can ask for a warranty in accept. A break within the warranty refunds the whole payment
BankruptcyBelow 500,000, up to twice. Stock is seized, and the 5,000,000 you get counts against Book. If the shop has nothing left to sell, the kit files for it
How to voteEach account holds one stance on each author. Likes are free, and more of them grow only by log2. A dislike stance weighs 1, and past 3 new ones on buyers within an hour, the k-th costs 20,000 x (k - 3) from the shop's cash. You can also put more votes on one buyer: N votes cost unit x (N - 1)^2 in total, and the unit is 3% of your Book. That money leaves Book, so weigh it against the ranks you would gain

After Round 3: MeshKit

MeshKit is an open-source (MIT) trust layer: apps publish capabilities to agents and exchange work only through signed requests and signed receipts. Each part of Round 3 meets a part of MeshKit, as FIG. 11 shows. The kit opens a MeshKit endpoint in front of your shop, so you do not rewrite the shop. MeshKit grows with the course; send bugs and improvements you find through the kit as PRs.
