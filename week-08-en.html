L1

The transaction stack and the missing layer

A trade splits into discovery, negotiation, execution, payment and record. Placing what we built in weeks 1 to 7 on these layers leaves only the payment layer empty.

LayerWeeksWhat happened in the week-07 market
DiscoveryW05 MCP, W06 agent card, W07 registryCards became ads aimed at AI buyers, including a forged "approved by the instructor"
NegotiationW03 Contract Net, W04 FIPA-ACL, W06 A2A taskUp to 75% of deals at the floor. A fake notice hidden in replies moved some buyers
ExecutionW01 agents, W02 harnessA buyer whose own model hit its rate limit gave shops 1 star 6,837 times
PaymentnoneNo credits moved. The budget was the delegation scope, yet it was public in the ledger
RecordW07 ledger, signed receipts30,198 forged receipts were refused. Colluding deals had valid signatures, so the record could not tell them apart

The week-07 order stood in for part of the payment layer. Item, budget, expiry and nonce were the scope the buyer could spend within. The market set that scope, anyone could read it, and the shop signed nothing before payment. ACP and AP2 fill this gap.

L2

ACP and AP2

ACPAgentic Commerce Protocol

A checkout specification by OpenAI and Stripe, released under Apache 2.0 and first implemented in ChatGPT. Versions are named by date.

VersionAdded
2025-09-29initial release
2025-12-12fulfillment enhancements
2026-01-16capability negotiation
2026-01-30extensions, discounts, payment handlers
2026-04-17carts, feeds, orders, delegate authentication, MCP transport binding (latest stable)

A checkout runs as one session.

ACP checkout (REST)
POST /checkout_sessions                 # create: line items in, totals out
GET  /checkout_sessions/{id}
POST /checkout_sessions/{id}            # update
POST /checkout_sessions/{id}/complete   # pay
POST /checkout_sessions/{id}/cancel

The MCP binding in the 2026-04-17 version exposes these five calls as the tools create_checkout_session, get_checkout_session, update_checkout_session, complete_checkout_session and cancel_checkout_session. A shop states what it supports in capabilities.services of /.well-known/acp.json. Capabilities such as payment methods and interventions are exchanged per session, with no separate registry. Payment credentials travel as a token that does not expose the underlying card; Stripe's Shared Payment Token is the first example.

AP2Agent Payments Protocol

Announced by Google on 2025-09-16; v0.2.0 came out on 2026-04-28. Standardization is moving to the FIDO Alliance. It is designed as an extension of A2A, MCP and UCP. The question AP2 answers is what proves that this agent may make this payment.

In v0.2 the proof is a mandate. There are Checkout Mandates and Payment Mandates, each Open or Closed.

Open (before the cart, signed by the user)Closed (bound to one checkout)
Checkoutmandate.checkout.open.1
checkout.allowed_merchants, checkout.line_items		mandate.checkout.1
merchant-signed checkout_jwt, checkout_hash
Paymentmandate.payment.open.1
payment.amount_range, payment.budget, payment.agent_recurrence, payment.allowed_payees, payment.execution_date		mandate.payment.1
amount and payee for that checkout

An open mandate is the delegation scope the user signs in advance on a Trusted Surface (an approval screen that is not an agent). Once a concrete deal exists, the Shopping Agent creates closed mandates inside that scope. A closed Checkout Mandate carries the merchant-signed checkout_jwt and its hash, so the merchant cannot later claim another price. The Payment Mandate is checked by the Credential Provider, the payment Network and the Merchant Payment Processor. The format is SD-JWT VC.

UCP (Universal Commerce Protocol, by Google, Shopify, Walmart and others) is another checkout specification in the same place as ACP. The AP2 documents say that with UCP, checkout_jwt carries UCP's Checkout object.

Three protocols named ACP
  • Agent Communication Protocol (IBM). An agent-to-agent protocol, merged into A2A under the Linux Foundation in 2025.
  • Agent Client Protocol. Between editors and coding agents: JSON-RPC over stdio, session/new, session/prompt, session/request_permission.
  • Agentic Commerce Protocol (OpenAI, Stripe). This week's ACP.

R3

Round 3: only settled deals count

What counts as a deal becomes a settlement, and products, stock and a forum are added (ECONOMY below). Negotiation is the A2A conversation we have used since week 6. The kit handles the checkout and settlement after it.

  1. Order. Every order comes with two open mandates, sent to the buyer only. Checkout holds the item; Payment holds amount_range (max = budget) and an execution deadline (the order's expiry). The market signs them, standing in for the user's Trusted Surface.
  2. Negotiation. Buyer and shop settle the price over A2A, and the shop's signed receipt comes out, as in week 7.
  3. Checkout. The buyer kit reads the shop's /.well-known/acp.json and opens a checkout session. The shop kit returns a checkout_jwt for the receipt's price and the SKU it will ship, signed with the shop key.
  4. Closed mandates. The buyer kit signs, with the buyer key, a closed Checkout Mandate holding checkout_jwt and its hash, and a closed Payment Mandate holding the amount and the payee shop.
  5. Settlement. When the buyer completes the checkout, the shop kit sends all mandates to the market's /api/settle. The market checks the signature chain and the constraints, then moves the credits.
  6. Record. Only settled deals count. In Round 3 the ledger shows a hash of the budget instead of the budget.
Week-07 incidentRound 3 check that stops it
Forged receiptsSettlement needs the shop-signed checkout_jwt to match checkout_hash in the closed Checkout Mandate
Over budget, reused ordersAmounts outside amount_range are refused. One settlement per order
Budgets in the ledgerThe budget lives only in the buyer's open mandate. The ledger keeps a hash
Fake notices in repliesThe payment amount comes from the checkout's structured totals. Chat text cannot change it

Where this differs from AP2 and ACP
  • Signatures are Ed25519 over canonical JSON, as in week 7, instead of SD-JWT VC. Field names and vct values follow AP2 v0.2.
  • Only part of the ACP checkout session endpoints is used. No tax, shipping or discounts.
  • One market plays the Trusted Surface, the Credential Provider and the payment Network.
